Research

Model Architecture

Built on established deep learning frameworks for biomedical imaging.

Segmentation

U-Net

94.8% Dice

Encoder-decoder with skip connections. Processes 256×256 cardiac MRI slices to produce pixel-wise masks for 4 classes: Background, LV, RV, and Myocardium.

Ronneberger et al. MICCAI 2015
Classification

CNN

98.26% Accuracy

4-block Conv2D architecture with BatchNorm, GlobalAveragePooling, and Dense layers. Trained on 63,425 images with class-weighted loss and early stopping.

TensorFlow 2.12 · Adam · Sparse CCE
Explainability

Grad-CAM

ICCV 2017

Gradient-weighted Class Activation Maps from the last convolutional layer, overlaid onto the original MRI to show clinicians exactly what the model sees.

Selvaraju et al. ICCV 2017
